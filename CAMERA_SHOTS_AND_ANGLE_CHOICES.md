# Camera Shots & Cinematography Choices

This guide governs shot composition and camera movement for Seedance 2.5 30-second vertical drama scenes.

---

## 1. Shot Typology & Emotional Impact

| Shot Type | Composition | Emotional Function | Best Timing Beat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wide Establishing (Living Room / Setting)** | Full environment, all active characters visible | Grounds spatial geography, establishes the celebratory illusion | 0.0–3.0s (Hook) |
| **Low-Angle Medium Close-Up** | Framed from chest up, camera tilted slightly upward | Conveys innocence, trust, or looking up to an authority figure | 3.0–8.0s (Innocent Beat) |
| **Over-the-Shoulder (OTS)** | Foreground character's shoulder/head framing the focal character | Builds conversational tension, establishes physical proximity | 8.0–13.0s (Reaction) |
| **Smooth Tracking Two-Shot** | Camera moves laterally keeping both subjects in frame | Bridges the distance before contact; builds suspense | 13.0–18.0s (Escalation) |
| **Macro Prop Close-Up (Insert)** | Tight focus on hands, fingers, and story-critical prop | Sensory contact trigger (`CAUSE -> CONTACT -> REACTION`) | 18.0–20.0s (Trigger Beat) |
| **Isolated Portrait Close-Up** | Head and shoulders, shallow depth of field | Isolates character from the group; reveals the psychological collapse | 20.0–25.0s (Turn) |
| **Extreme Portrait Macro / Bokeh Lock** | Tight on eyes and face, background blurred into bokeh | Climax of internal grief/betrayal; holds a single tear cliffhanger | 25.0–30.0s (Cliffhanger) |

---

## 2. Inviolable Cinematography Rules
1. **Never flip screen direction:** If Character A is on screen-left in Clip 1, they must remain on screen-left unless an explicit tracking walk is shown on camera.
2. **No erratic camera shakes:** Use controlled slow push-ins or gentle floating camera. Handheld camera is permitted only during high emotional panic.
3. **Lighting continuity:** When transitioning between consecutive clips, the ambient lighting, window shadows, and color temperature must remain identical.
