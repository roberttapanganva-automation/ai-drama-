# Drama Playbook: The Counterpoint Betrayal Engine

This playbook outlines the narrative mechanics for building high-tension 30-second episodic family drama scenes without creating repetitive or formulaic content.

---

## 1. The Core Tension Mechanics: Counterpoint & Dramatic Irony

The highest viral engagement in short-form drama occurs when **surface bliss directly collides with private devastation**.

### The 3 Core Archetypes:
1. **The Joyful Innocent:** A child, naive spouse, or trusting parent experiencing an untainted milestone (birthday, acceptance letter, new keys, award, vow renewal).
2. **The Oblivious Anchor:** A family member or friend celebrating and enjoying the moment, amplifying the emotional warmth and security of the scene.
3. **The Compromised Protagonist:** The character carrying the secret catastrophe (an affair, financial embezzlement, a signed divorce petition, impending foreclosure, a sacrifice).

---

## 2. The 7-Beat 30-Second Dramatic Rhythm

```text
0.0–3.0s   | INSTANT HOOK        | The celebration or milestone is immediately established at peak joy.
3.0–8.0s   | THE INNOCENT BEAT   | The innocent character expresses adoration, gratitude, or achievement.
8.0–13.0s  | OBLIVIOUS REINFORCE | The anchor character celebrates, cementing the illusion of security.
13.0–18.0s | INTIMATE CONVERGE   | The protagonist steps in, hands and gaze meeting around the story prop.
18.0–20.0s | THE TACTILE TRIGGER | A physical sensory action (a clinking bell, a key drop, a glass clink).
20.0–25.0s | THE FACADE COLLAPSE | The protagonist’s smile dies; world muffles; camera isolates them.
25.0–30.0s | THE CLIFFHANGER     | Extreme portrait lock; single tear or stoic grief held into blackness.
```

---

## 3. Idea Generator: 5 Distinct Family Betrayal Archetypes

To ensure content remains fresh and never repetitive, rotate through these story premises:

1. **The Forged Future:** Celebrating a child's university/career breakthrough using funds the parent secretly stole or gambled away.
2. **The Stolen Milestone:** An elaborate wedding anniversary party where one partner has already served divorce/custody papers secretly dated for tomorrow morning.
3. **The Blood-Money Inheritance:** An adult child presenting their elderly parent with a gift or medical cure acquired by selling out the family business to their bitter rival.
4. **The Ghost Family:** A husband playing the doting patriarch at his daughter's recital while receiving secret blackmail texts from his second family in another city.
5. **The Sacrificial Frame:** A sibling accepting praise for saving the family company, knowing they framed their other sibling to take the criminal fallout.
